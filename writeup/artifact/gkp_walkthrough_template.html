Keep these three ideas separate
State, disturbance, observation.
Δ
The state has finite energy
Δ controls the encoder's energy envelope. It changes the shape of the quantum state. It is not automatically inserted into the simulator's displacement or measurement noise.
dₜ → xₜ
A kick changes the residual
dₜ is this round's displacement. xₜ also depends on previous displacements and corrections. The decoder sees measurements, not the true kick or residual plotted in diagnostics.
sₜ = wrap(x + νₜ)
The observation hides a cell index
A syndrome gives an offset modulo L. Several possible displacements produce the same syndrome. History can change which logical parity is most plausible.
